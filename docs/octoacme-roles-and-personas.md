# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing

### Role Summary
QA / Testing validates that delivered work meets acceptance criteria and quality expectations. The role helps the team identify issues early and make release readiness visible.

### Responsibilities
- Turn acceptance criteria into test scenarios and an appropriate test approach
- Validate features, document results, and report reproducible defects
- Verify fixes and communicate remaining quality risks before release
- Coordinate smoke testing and other release checks with the delivery team

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers to clarify acceptance criteria; Product Managers remain accountable for product decisions and acceptance.
- Coordinate testability and defect fixes with Developers; Developers own implementation and code fixes.
- Share test progress, defects, and release risks with Project Managers for tracking and escalation.

### Goals
- Find issues before they affect users
- Provide clear evidence of quality and remaining risks
- Support predictable, well-informed releases

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, test results, and reproducible defect reports
- Quality and release-readiness updates during delivery reviews

## Project Sponsors

### Role Summary
Project Sponsors provide organizational direction and support for a project. They are accountable for strategic alignment and timely decisions on escalated business or resource trade-offs.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm the project’s business rationale and intended outcomes
- Secure or escalate the resources and organizational support the project needs
- Make or obtain decisions on escalated scope, priority, and risk trade-offs
- Remove organizational impediments beyond the delivery team’s authority

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Align with Product Managers on desired outcomes and business priorities; Product Managers continue to own product direction and backlog prioritization.
- Rely on Project Managers for concise status, risk, and decision requests; Project Managers continue to coordinate day-to-day delivery.
- Provide decisions and escalation support without directing Developers’ implementation work.

### Goals
- Keep the project aligned with organizational priorities
- Resolve high-impact decisions and impediments promptly
- Ensure the team has appropriate support to deliver outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Project charter or business case reviews
- Milestone updates and escalations requiring sponsor decisions

---

## Operations / Support Representatives

### Role Summary
Operations / Support Representatives bring operational and customer-support needs into project planning and coordinate readiness for changes that affect their teams.

### Responsibilities
- Identify operational requirements, support impacts, and service dependencies
- Review user-facing changes for supportability and readiness
- Prepare or coordinate support guidance, training, and operational handoffs
- Raise readiness gaps and post-release concerns to the delivery team

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Share customer and operational needs with Product Managers to inform requirements and prioritization.
- Coordinate readiness activities, dependencies, and handoffs with Project Managers, who track them in the project plan.
- Work with Developers and QA / Testing to clarify operational behavior and validate support-critical scenarios.
- Own operational and support readiness for their teams, not product scope or delivery schedule decisions.

### Goals
- Prepare teams to support changes when they are released
- Reduce avoidable customer impact and support escalations
- Make operational dependencies visible before release

### Typical Communication
- Support-impact reviews and operational readiness checklists
- Release briefings, support guidance, and post-release feedback
